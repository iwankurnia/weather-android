Android 16 look (Material 3 Expressive)

The content is the same as the iPhone-style screen, restyled the way Android 16 apps look: bold, wide Roboto Flex numbers, solid rounded cards instead of frosted glass, playful shapes (the "feels like" cookie, the UV sun), pill-shaped hourly items, and the grouped list from Android 16 Settings for the 10-day forecast. The background still comes from the weather and the time of day.
